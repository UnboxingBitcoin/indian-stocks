How it works

  • Prices: live from CoinGecko (Coinbase as backup), refreshed every minute.
  • Cube levels on the USD price: primary n³ (1 above, 1 below), secondary n³/10 and tertiary n³/100 (3 above, 3 below). Coins under $100 shift one step: n³/10, n³/100, n³/1000.
  • Square of 9 (√P ± 0.5, 1, 2)² and Square of 12 (√P ± 1/6, 1/3, 2/3)².
  • Pivot windows: Gann counts from 30 to 1440 days and weeks from weekly, daily, all-time and 52-week highs and lows, grouped where 3 or more land within 4 days.
  • Astro history: Swiss Ephemeris, Lahiri sidereal; a hit is a ±10-day turn within 2 days of the event.
  • Gann data refreshes daily at about 6:10 pm IST.
Not investment advice. Levels are mechanical Gann calculations.
