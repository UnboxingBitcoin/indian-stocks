How levels work

  • Primary cube n³: 1 level above, 1 below.
  • Secondary cube n³/10: 3 above, 3 below.
  • Tertiary cube n³/100: 3 above, 3 below.
  • Stocks under the cheap-stock price (set in the sheet, ₹100 now) shift down one step: n³/10, n³/100, n³/1000.
  • Square of 9: (√P ± 0.5, 1, 2)² for 90°, 180°, 360°.
  • Square of 12: (√P ± 1/6, 1/3, 2/3)² for 30°, 60°, 120°.
  • The cube signal checks primary, then secondary, then tertiary, within the near-level band (1% now).

Data

Prices come from Google Finance through the screener's Google Sheet, delayed up to about 20 minutes. Index lists are NSE's official constituents (Nov 2026). The page refreshes every 5 minutes while open.
Pivot windows and astro history come from a nightly job (Mon–Fri, about 6:10 pm IST) that reads every NSE stock's full daily history.
How pivot windows work
  • Major pivots: weekly swing highs and lows (highest or lowest within ±13 weeks, full history), daily swings (±20 days, last 3 years), all-time and 52-week high and low.
  • From each pivot the job counts 30, 45, 49, 60, 63, 64, 72, 84, 90, 98, 100, 108, 120, 135, 144, 147, 180, 196, 216, 225, 240, 270, 288, 315, 343, 360, 432, 512, 540, 576, 720, 729, 1000, 1080 and 1440 days and weeks ahead.
  • Dates within 4 days of each other are grouped into one window. Score = number of cycles, with weekly and all-time pivots and week counts weighted higher.
How astro history works
  • Events since 2005 from Swiss Ephemeris, Lahiri sidereal for sign changes: eclipses, new and full moons, stations, ingress of Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu and the outer planets, and aspects between the slow planets.
  • A hit = the stock made a turning point (highest or lowest within ±10 days) within 2 trading days of the event.
  • Base = how often any random day is that close to a turning point for the same stock. Only categories with 5+ past events are shown. A rate well above base, over many events, is what matters.
