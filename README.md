# India Gann Screener

Live page: https://unboxingbitcoin.github.io/indian-stocks/ (home) · india.html · crypto.html

A phone-first screener for NSE/BSE indices and their stocks with Gann cube, square, Square of 9 and Square of 12 levels. Prices and level calculations come from the "India Gann Screener" Google Sheet (GOOGLEFINANCE), which must be shared as "Anyone with the link: Viewer".
